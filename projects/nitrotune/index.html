Home GitHub

Get the fans on your Acer laptop to work like they were meant to. Your laptop is quiet, then it sounds like a hairdryer, all while you're on a zoom call for work. Fix it for good with NitroTune.

Stop noticing the noise, while your laptop maintains a cooler idle.

See What It Is Doing

Temperature, fan speed, memory and battery are now all trackable on screen — readings Linux never showed you.

If Something Goes Wrong

If NitroTune stops, the laptop takes back control of its own fans. If it cannot read the temperature, the fans go up rather than down. Nothing is left to chance.
