Create a padded room for your favourite AI agent, so it can work unsupervised on your computer without putting your files in jeopardy.

Nothing Left To Get Wrong

Doing this by hand was time consuming, creating many opportunities for mistakes. Now you point Capsule at a folder and answer a few questions. Everything else is worked out for you.

Nothing is built until it has shown you its plan and you have approved it.

Checked Before You Trust It

Setup finishes by opening the live room and testing that it is truly isolated and secure. You watch the result of each check, and then you're ready to work.

Getting it

Capsule is free software. The source is on GitHub, and the repository is the whole distribution — there is no installer, no store listing and no account.
