Renault Master Camper Conversion

Home Projects

A once AusPost van became my blank canvas when I built it into a fully livable home on wheels, including a kitchen, bed, heating, power, water and a toilet all functioning within the tight space. Upon completion I put the campervan to the test by driving 8,800 km to Darwin and back. It has since sold, and I hope its new owner is getting good use out of it.
