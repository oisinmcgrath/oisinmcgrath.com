Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy covers this website — what happens when you read a page here. Each application listed under Projects is separate software with its own privacy policy, and those policies govern the applications, not this site.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, no embedded third-party widget, and no cookie set by this site. No account can be created here and no form on this site submits anything anywhere.

What the site loads

Every file a page needs — stylesheets, images, video, marks and the two small scripts described below — is served from oisinmcgrath.com itself. There are no content delivery networks, no external font services and no requests to any other domain while you read a page. Opening a page here does not tell anyone else that you did.

Scripts

The site is hand-written HTML and CSS and carries exactly two scripts, both a few lines long, both first-party, and neither of which stores anything or contacts the network:

  • The back button (/back.js) — returns you to the page you came from, when that page was on this site.
  • The unmute button on the Cadence page — turns the sound on for the demo video, because browsers will only start a video on their own if it is silent.

Nothing on this site uses localStorage, session storage or any other browser storage. The light and dark theme switch on the homepage and the projects index is CSS; it deliberately does not remember your choice, because remembering it would mean storing it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, your browser has to reach the host to fetch a page, so Cloudflare necessarily processes connection data — such as your IP address, the page requested and the time — in order to serve the site and to protect it from abuse. That processing is Cloudflare's and is governed by their privacy policy. I have not added any analytics product to this site and receive no report identifying who visits it.

Links away from here

Pages here link to GitHub, LinkedIn, Facebook and the South West Irish Community Group's own site. Following a link takes you to a service with its own policies and its own tracking, over which this site has no control and from which it receives nothing.

Photographs of other people

The community page shows photographs of volunteers and attendees at a public festival, published to credit the group. If you appear in one and would rather not, email me and I will remove it — no explanation needed.

If you email me

Then I have your email address and whatever you wrote, in my mailbox, for as long as I keep the message. I use it to reply to you and for nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list, because there is no mailing list.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The site is a static repository, so the history of what it said is a matter of record.
